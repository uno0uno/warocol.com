# User guide

Welcome to the WARO documentation. Here you’ll find everything you need to run your restaurant day to day.

The docs mirror the sidebar structure: Getting started, then Main, Tools, Account, and Dev.

---

## Getting started

- [Getting started](./usuarios/primeros-pasos) — login and recommended order to begin

---

## Main

| Module | What you can do |
|--------|------------------|
| [POS](./usuarios/pos) | Process sales at the point of sale (counter, table, bar); includes [open sale](./usuarios/pos-venta-libre) |
| [Sales](./usuarios/ventas) | Order history, products sold, and tips |
| [Dispatch](./usuarios/despacho) | Online orders (delivery and pickup) and kitchen tickets |

## Tools

| Module | What you can do |
|--------|------------------|
| [Sales Analytics](./usuarios/analitica) | Dashboards for sales, profitability, customers, and points |
| [Finance](./usuarios/finanzas) | Cash count, accounting close, receivables, expenses, payment methods, and accounting |
| [Billing](./usuarios/facturacion) | DIAN electronic invoicing, resolution, tax data |
| [Menu](./usuarios/menu) | Recipes, products, modifiers, and categories |
| [Operations](./usuarios/operaciones) | Tickets & kitchen, tables, customization, and tips |
| [Procurement](./usuarios/abastecimiento) | Suppliers, purchases, stock, adjustments, in-house ingredients, and data quality |
| [Team](./usuarios/equipo) | Members, salaries, payroll, and waiter profiles |

## Account

| Module | What you can do |
|--------|------------------|
| [My Business](./usuarios/negocio) | Public profile, hours, contact, and online order settings |
| [My Plan](./usuarios/mi-plan) | Subscription, payment history, and AI usage |

## Dev

| Resource | What for |
|---------|----------|

| [CLI](./cli) | Command-line tools |
