# Record manual sale

Use this option when a sale happened outside the POS: advance payment, phone order, courtesy recorded later, etc.

## When to use it

- The customer paid before arriving at the venue
- The sale was processed on paper and needs to be entered in the system
- You need to record a sale on a past date

---

## Steps

### 1. Date, payment method, and customer

- Select the **date** the sale occurred (cannot be a future date)
- Choose the **payment method**. The list uses methods configured for the POS
- If the method has sub-methods (for example Nequi or Daviplata), select it within the corresponding group
- Optionally tap **Identify customer** to associate the sale with a registered customer

If you identify a customer with a balance in **COP Wallet**, the wallet appears as an available payment method. If the sale stays anonymous or the customer has no balance, that method is not shown.

### 2. Add products

The catalog shows all your available products.

- Tap a product to add it to the cart
- If the product has **Modifiers**, a side panel appears to select them
- Use the **+** / **−** buttons to adjust quantities directly in the cart

### 3. Confirm

The right panel (desktop) or bottom summary (mobile) shows the total in real time.

Press **Record sale** to save. The sale will be recorded with the date you chose and inventory is deducted automatically if the products have recipes configured.

---

## Frequently asked questions

**Is inventory deducted?**
Yes, just like in the POS. If the product has a recipe, warehouse items are deducted when you record the sale.

**Can I record a sale without identifying the customer?**
Yes, the customer is optional for manual sales.

**Can I use a date from several days ago?**
Yes, you can select any past date. The sale will appear in that day's reports.

**Can I record a tip or assign a waiter on a manual sale?**
No. Manual sales do not support tips or waiter attribution. For those cases use the POS, which records both fields at checkout.
