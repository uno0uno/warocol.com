# POS

The **POS** (point of sale) is where you take in-person orders — counter, table, or bar — add products to the cart, and collect payment.

## How to access

Sidebar → **POS**.

- If tables are disabled, you go straight to products.
- If they’re enabled, you’ll see the floor plan first.
