# Dispatch

**Dispatch** centralizes online orders, the queue of table QR orders pending confirmation, and, if applicable, the kitchen ticket monitor.

## How to access

Sidebar → **Dispatch**. Depending on your setup you’ll see up to three tabs:

| Tab | What for |
|-----|----------|
| **Delivery** | Online orders (delivery, pickup, dine-in) |
| **Table orders (QR)** | QR orders pending accept or reject |
| **Tickets** | Live kitchen monitor (if tickets are enabled) |
