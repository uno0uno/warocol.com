# Procurement

From **Procurement** you manage what enters inventory: suppliers, purchases (with AI or formal), stock, adjustments, warehouse catalog, and data quality.

## How to access

Sidebar → **Procurement**. Main tabs:

| Tab | What for |
|-----|----------|
| **Suppliers** | Directory and payment terms |
| **Direct purchase** | Paid invoices with AI scanner |
| **Stock** | Levels, KPIs, and alerts |
| **Adjustments** | Manual inventory corrections |
| **Warehouse catalog** | Restaurant’s own items |

You’ll also see **Data Quality**; formal purchase orders and supplier payments open from contextual actions.
