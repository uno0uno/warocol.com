# My Business

From **My Business** you control the restaurant’s public profile: how you appear on WARO, contact, hours, and online orders.

## How to access

Sidebar → **Account → My Business**. Use **Edit profile** to change and **Save** to apply.

## What you can configure

- Public profile (name, description, logo, banner)
- Directory visibility and public link
- Contact, hours, and social networks
- Online orders (delivery / pickup)
