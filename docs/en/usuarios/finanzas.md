# Finance

**Finance** groups money control: cash count, accounting close, credit receivables, expenses, payment methods, and accounting reports.

## How to access

Sidebar → **Finance**. Main tabs:

| Tab | What for |
|-----|----------|
| **Cash count** | Daily reconciliation (Z close) and preview (X close) |
| **Accounting close** | Irreversible monthly close |
| **Receivables** | Pending customer credits |
| **Expenses** | Operating costs |
| **Payment methods** | Groups and methods in POS and reports |

You’ll also find **Accounts**, **Entries**, **Balance**, and **Monthly P&L** for tenant accounting.
