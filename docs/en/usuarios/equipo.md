# Team

From **Team** you manage who has access, with which role, salaries, payments, and social benefits.

## How to access

Sidebar → **Team**. Tabs:

- **Members** — access, roles, and waiter profiles
- **Salaries** — configure salary and ongoing payments
- **Payroll** — benefits (Prima, Cesantías, etc.) and PILA
