# Configure an employee's salary

Before recording payments, each employee needs to have their salary configured. WaRo supports three payment schemes.

## Access

Sidebar → **Team** → **Salaries** → employee configuration icon

---

## Salary types

### 1. Based on SMMLV

The salary is calculated as a multiple of the current legal monthly minimum wage.

- Enter the **multiplier** (e.g. `1.5` for 1.5 SMMLV)
- Range: 0.5× to 10×
- Quick access: 1× · 1.5× · 2× · 2.5× · 3×
- The system uses the current year's SMMLV value to calculate the amount

### 2. Fixed amount

Define a fixed monthly amount in Colombian pesos.

- Quick access: $1.5M · $2M · $2.5M · $3M · $4M

### 3. Hourly

Define an hourly rate. The monthly salary calculation depends on recorded hours.

- Quick access: $5K · $8K · $10K · $15K · $20K per hour

---

## Payment frequency

| Option | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Monthly | 1 payment per month |
| Biweekly | 2 payments per month |
| Weekly | 4 payments per month |

The **side summary** shows the total monthly salary and the amount of each payment in real time according to the chosen frequency.

---

## Notes

Optional field to record observations about the salary agreement (bonuses, additional benefits, etc.).

---

## Save

Press **Configure salary**. The salary becomes active immediately and appears in the monthly payroll in the salaries module.
