# Add an employee

## Why register employees in WARO?

Registering your team allows you to:
- Control who has access to which parts of the system
- Manage salaries and payments from the platform
- Keep a trace of who recorded each operation

---

## How to invite a team member

Go to **Team → Members → Invite member**.

Enter the person's email address. WARO will send them an email with a link to create their password and access the system.

### Available roles

| Role | What they can do |
|-----|-----------------|
| **Super user** | Full access, including role configuration and billing |
| **Administrator** | Full access to all operational sections |
| **Employee** | Limited access (mainly POS and basic operations) |
| **Member** | Minimal access. Useful for staff who appear as waiters but do not operate the platform |

> The role can be changed later from the members list.

---

## How to configure an employee's salary

After adding the employee, go to **Team → Salaries** and search for their name.

### Salary types

| Type | When to use it |
|------|---------------|
| **Fixed** | The employee always earns the same amount per period |
| **SMMLV** | Salary is a multiple of the minimum wage (e.g. 1.5x SMMLV) |
| **Hourly** | Paid according to hours worked at an hourly rate |

Configure the type and amount, then save. This serves as a reference when you record payments.

---

## How to change an employee's role

Go to **Team → Members**, find the employee and click the edit role icon (only available to administrators).

## How to view a member's profile

In the members list, each row has an **eye** icon ("View profile") that opens the individual waiter profile. There you can see the tips they have received (Today, last 7 and 30 days) and their latest orders with tips. Tip information only appears if tips are enabled in **Operations → Tips**.

---

## Frequently asked questions

**Can the employee see all business information?**
It depends on the role. An employee with the "Employee" role has restricted access. An "Admin" sees everything.

**What if the employee does not receive the invitation email?**
Ask them to check their spam folder. If it still does not arrive, you can resend the invitation from the members list.

**Can I remove an employee?**
Yes, from the members list. The employee loses access immediately. Their operation history is preserved.

**Can I have multiple administrators?**
Yes, there is no limit on administrators.
