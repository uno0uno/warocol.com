# Record salary payment

## Why record payments in WARO?

Recording salary payments lets you keep a clear history of when you paid each employee, how much, and for which period. It also lets you attach receipts.

---

## How to record a payment

Go to **Team → Salaries**, find the employee and click their name. Then select **Record payment**.

### Payment information

| Field | What to enter | Required |
|-------|-----------|:--------:|
| Payment amount | Amount in Colombian pesos | Yes |
| Payment date | The day the payment was made | Yes |
| Payment method | Cash, transfer, check, or other | Yes |
| Reference | Transfer number or bank receipt | No |
| Period | The month and year this salary corresponds to | Yes |

> If the employee has a configured salary, WARO shows you the reference amount for that period. You can use it as a base or enter a different value.

### Receipts

You can attach files (photos, PDFs) as payment support. This is useful for backup if the payment was by transfer.

Click **Record payment** to save.

---

## How to view payment history

Go to **Team → Salaries** and select the employee. You will see all recorded payments with date, amount, and period.

You can also go to **Team → Salaries → Payments** to see all team payments in one place.

---

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I record a partial payment?**
Yes. The amount is flexible; it does not have to match the configured salary exactly. Record the actual amount you paid.

**Can I delete a recorded payment?**
Check with the administrator. Payments are accounting records and are generally not deleted; instead, a correction note is added.

**Does the employee receive a payment notification?**
Not automatically. If you need to send them the receipt, attach it and share it separately.

**Does WARO automatically calculate social benefits?**
The system records payments, but the calculation of benefits (Cesantías, vacation, etc.) must be done by you or your accountant.
