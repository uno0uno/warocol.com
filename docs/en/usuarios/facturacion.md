# Billing

From **Billing** you configure what’s needed to issue electronic invoices valid with DIAN: resolution, tax data, taxes, and technical provider.

## How to access

Sidebar → **Billing**. You’ll see a readiness banner and cards to complete the setup.

## What to review

- **DIAN resolution** — authorized range and validity
- **Tax data** — business identity
- **Taxes applied to sales** — under WARO’s guardrail
- **Technical provider** — electronic issuance
- **Burned numbers log** — consecutive number control
