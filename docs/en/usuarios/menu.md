# Menu

From **Menu** you set up what you offer customers: base recipes, menu products, and modifiers (sizes, extras, sauces).

## How to access

Sidebar → **Menu**.

## Next

- [Recipes](./menu/recetas) — reusable compositions of warehouse items
- [Products](./menu/productos) — what appears in POS and delivery
- [Create product](./menu/crear-producto) — step-by-step setup
- [Modifiers](./menu/modificadores) — options to customize products

> Before you sell, the menu is the critical step: without products you can’t run POS or online orders.
