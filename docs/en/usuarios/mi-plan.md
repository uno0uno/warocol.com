# My Plan

From **My Plan** you manage your WARO subscription: current plan, payments, history, and AI usage.

## How to access

Sidebar → **Account → My Plan**. Tabs:

| Tab | What for |
|-----|----------|
| **History** | Current plan, subscription, payments, and events |
| **AI usage** | Monthly AI scan consumption |
