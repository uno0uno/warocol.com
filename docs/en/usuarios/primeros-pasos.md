# Getting started

Welcome to WARO COLOMBIA. This guide explains how to sign in and where to begin.

WARO COLOMBIA started by helping a real restaurant in Bogotá organize its daily operations. Today it’s the platform for your business to sell, collect payment, and control day-to-day work without friction.

---

## How to sign in

WARO COLOMBIA doesn’t use passwords. Access is via a **magic link**: every time you want to sign in, we send a one-time code to your email.

### Step by step

1. Go to [warocol.com](https://warocol.com/auth/login) and click **Sign in**
2. Enter your email address and click **Next**
3. Check your inbox — you’ll get a message with two ways to sign in:

**Option A — Direct link:**
Click the button in the email. Your browser opens and you’re signed in automatically.

**Option B — 6-digit code:**
Copy the code from the email, paste it on the WARO COLOMBIA screen, and click **Verify Code**.

> The link and code are valid for **15 minutes**. If they expire, go back to the login screen and request a new one with "Resend".

### If you don’t have an account yet

Open **Create account** from the sign-in screen or go to [registro](/registro). Enter your email, WhatsApp country code and number, accept consent, and verify your email with the link or the 6-digit code.

After verification, WARO COLOMBIA opens your business’s initial setup. If you interrupt the process, you can sign in again with the same email to continue from the pending step. Registration doesn’t require a call or human onboarding.

## Recommended order to get started

If you just created your account, follow this order to get your operation running:

**1. Set up your Menu (critical step)**

Creating your menu is required to enable POS and online orders.

- [Menu](/docs/usuarios/menu) — create your recipes, products, and modifiers before selling anything.

**2. Start selling**

With products ready, you can operate:

- [POS](/docs/usuarios/pos) — take in-person orders and collect payment at the register.
- [Dispatch](/docs/usuarios/despacho) — manage delivery and pickup orders.
- [Tables](/docs/usuarios/operaciones/mesas) — if you have a dining room, manage tables and active sessions.

**3. Control your finances**

Once you sell, keep track of the money:

- [Finance](/docs/usuarios/finanzas) — customer credit receivables, expenses, daily accounting close, and payment methods.
- [Sales](/docs/usuarios/ventas) — full order history and reports.
- [Analytics](/docs/usuarios/analitica) — sales metrics, best sellers, and trends.

**4. Optimize procurement**

When you want a clear view of your costs:

- [Procurement](/docs/usuarios/abastecimiento) — register invoices with AI, manage suppliers and purchase orders.

**5. Manage your team**

Set up access and payroll:

- [Team](/docs/usuarios/equipo) — invite members, assign roles, configure salaries, and record payments.

## Sign out

Your session stays active while you use the platform. To sign out manually, click your name or avatar at the bottom of the sidebar and select **Sign out**.

---

## If something doesn’t work

- **Email didn’t arrive** — check your spam folder. If it’s not there, click "Resend" on the login screen.
- **Code doesn’t work** — it may have expired. Request a new one.
- **Can’t sign in** — contact the WARO COLOMBIA team to confirm your email is enabled.
