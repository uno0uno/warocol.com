# Sales

From **Sales** you browse order history, filter and export, and can record sales that happened outside the POS.

## How to access

Sidebar → **Sales**. Tabs:

- **Orders** — general history (default view)
- **Products** — what was sold and in what quantity
- **Tips** — tip history (settings live in Operations)
