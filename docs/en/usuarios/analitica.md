# Analytics

**Analytics** shows you in numbers how the business is doing: sales, top products, customers, and profitability.

## How to access

Sidebar → **Sales Analytics**. You land on the **Sales** view; from there you can also go to:

- **Profitability** — margins by product
- **Customers** — frequency and behavior
- **Points** — points program (Waros)
